A namespace named explicitly wins over a label selector; otherwise, the first matching policy in the list. A namespace without a policy follows the default policy. The HYCU policy is assigned to volumes that do not have one yet.

Saved versions

Bulk restore

Recreates every deleted namespace of the active cluster () from its latest backup prior to the reference time: namespace, PV/PVC, workloads, dependencies, Secrets (vault unlocked), Volume Groups restored by HYCU if they are gone, stateless applications. Namespaces still present are skipped: a live application is restored from its own row. Sequential, journaled, resumable; simulation is mandatory before the real run.

Set Policy

2Protect the data in HYCU

The data lives in Nutanix Volume Groups or on NAS shares: only HYCU actually backs it up. Here the namespace's PVCs are matched to their HYCU Volume Groups or shares, a HYCU policy is assigned, and a backup is started.
